# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

DiffusionEngine is an open-source project focused on building a modular, extensible, and research-oriented diffusion framework using PyTorch.

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone who participates in the project, regardless of their background, experience level, technical expertise, or identity.

We believe that open-source development thrives through constructive collaboration, knowledge sharing, and mutual respect.

## Expected Behavior

All contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to:

- Treat everyone with respect, professionalism, and consideration.
- Communicate constructively and provide technical feedback in a respectful manner.
- Welcome different perspectives, approaches, and levels of experience.
- Accept constructive criticism and engage in technical discussions objectively.
- Share knowledge, resources, and ideas that help improve the project.
- Give appropriate credit to original authors, researchers, and contributors.
- Respect project guidelines, contribution standards, and maintainers' decisions.
- Focus discussions on improving the codebase, architecture, documentation, and research quality.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, humiliating, or deliberately discouraging other contributors.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or persistent hostile communication.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Plagiarism, misrepresentation of contributions, or claiming others' work as one's own.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, misleading documentation, or harmful changes.
- Any other conduct that creates an unwelcoming or hostile environment.

Disagreements regarding implementation choices, architectural decisions, research methodologies, or technical direction are expected. However, disagreements must remain professional and focused on the subject rather than the individual.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, and pull requests.
- Discussions and technical reviews.
- Documentation and contribution channels.
- Project-related communication and community spaces.
- Any other environment where an individual represents DiffusionEngine or its community.

## Reporting Issues

Instances of unacceptable behavior should be reported directly to the project maintainers through private communication on GitHub.

Reports should include relevant details, such as the nature of the incident, individuals involved, and supporting evidence where available.

All reports will be reviewed seriously and handled with appropriate discretion.

Maintainers are responsible for protecting the privacy of individuals involved to the extent reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions:

1. **Warning:** A private or public warning requesting that the behavior stop.
2. **Temporary Restriction:** Temporary restriction from participating in discussions, reviews, or contributions.
3. **Removal:** Removal of inappropriate content or contributions.
4. **Temporary Suspension:** Temporary suspension from project participation.
5. **Permanent Ban:** Permanent removal from the project community for severe or repeated violations.

Maintainers reserve the right to determine the appropriate response based on the circumstances of each incident.

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

Project maintainers are expected to:

- Apply these standards consistently and fairly.
- Encourage constructive technical discussions.
- Address reported violations promptly and responsibly.
- Avoid favoritism or discriminatory treatment.
- Maintain a professional and respectful project environment.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in DiffusionEngine, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and contribute toward maintaining a respectful, collaborative, and productive open-source community.

We welcome contributions from everyone who shares an interest in diffusion models, generative AI, PyTorch, and open-source research.
